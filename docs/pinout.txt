Bottom edge (left→right):

  GP12   RS485_TX
  GP11   RS485_DIR
  GP10   BUZZER
  GP9    CAMERA

Bottom-side SMD pads (top→bottom):
  GND    GND
  GP25   DBG
  GP24   DBG
  GP23   DBG
  GP22   DBG
  GP21   DBG
  GP20   DBG
  GP19   DBG
  GP18   DBG
  GP17   DBG

DMX_TX GP0 (PIO UART 250k 8N2). No DMX levels on servo PWM.
SERVO_1..8 hobby PWM on GP1..GP8. RS485 256000 baud: TX GP12, RX GP13, DIR GP11.
CAMERA GP9 (OC). BUZZER GP10. GIO OUT GP29..GP26. GIO IN GP15, GP14.
GP16 = internal WS2812 status LED. GP17..GP25 are DBG pads (inputs, free for probes).
